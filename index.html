Best SIP Finder

Direct Growth funds ko CAGR (5Y, naye funds ka 3Y/1Y), Exit Load aur Expense Ratio ke rank se sort karta hai.

Upar se category choose karo.

Ranking kaise hoti hai

1. Shabdon ka matlab

CAGR (Compound Annual Growth Rate)
Fund ne har saal average kitna return diya. Jaise 5Y CAGR 15% matlab pichhle 5 saal mein paisa har saal ~15% ki speed se badha (₹1 lakh lagbhag ₹2 lakh ban gaya). Zyada CAGR = acha. Ye NAV (ek unit ki keemat) se nikala jata hai: aaj ka NAV vs 5 saal pehle ka NAV.
Exit Load
Agar aap paisa jaldi nikalte ho to fund jo penalty kaatta hai. Jaise "1% < 365d" matlab 1 saal ke andar nikala to 1% kategi; ₹1,00,000 nikale to ₹1,000 kat jayenge. 1 saal ke baad koi charge nahi. "(10% free)" matlab har saal 10% units bina charge ke nikal sakte ho. "Nil" matlab koi exit load nahi. Kam exit load = acha.
Expense Ratio
Fund ko chalane ki saalana fees, jo har saal aapke paise mein se chupchap kat jati hai. Jaise 0.75% matlab ₹1 lakh par saal ka ~₹750. Ye har saal lagta hai, isliye lambe samay mein bada farak padta hai. Kam expense ratio = acha.
Direct Plan + Growth
Direct plan mein koi distributor/agent commission nahi hota, isliye expense ratio Regular plan se kam hota hai. Growth option mein munafa wapas fund mein lag jata hai (compounding), payout nahi hota.
Min SIP
Har mahine kam se kam kitne rupaye ki SIP shuru kar sakte ho.

2. Ranking ke steps

  1. Category choose karo: Large Cap, Mid Cap ya Small Cap.
  2. Fund list: us category ke saare Direct Plan + Growth funds liye jaate hain (AMFI ki official list se).
  3. Har fund ke 3 rank banaye jaate hain:
    • CAGR rank: sabse zyada CAGR wala = rank 1.
    • Exit load rank: sabse kam exit load wala = rank 1. Pehle % dekha jata hai (kam acha); % same ho to period (chhota acha, jaise 30 din < 1 saal); vo bhi same ho to free units (zyada acha). Nil sabse upar. Agar do level ka load ho (jaise 30 din tak 0.25%, 90 din tak 0.10%) to zyada wala % aur lamba period liya jata hai.
    • Expense ratio rank: sabse kam expense ratio wala = rank 1.
  4. Total = CAGR rank + Exit load rank + Expense ratio rank.
  5. Final rank: jiska Total sabse kam, vo rank 1 (best). Rank jitna chhota utna acha, isliye teeno ka jod bhi jitna chhota, fund utna balanced aur acha. Top 3 funds peele (highlight) dikhte hain.

4. Barabari (tie) ho to

  • Do funds ki value same ho to dono ko same rank milta hai, aur agla rank skip hota hai: 1, 1, 3 (2 nahi). Jaise do funds ka exit load dono "1% < 365d" hai to dono ka exit load rank same hoga.
  • Do funds ka Total same ho to dono ko same final rank milta hai; table mein zyada CAGR wala upar dikhta hai.

5. Naye aur khaas funds

  • 5 saal purana fund: 5Y CAGR. 3 se 5 saal: 3Y CAGR. 1 se 3 saal: 1Y CAGR. 3Y/1Y orange mein dikhte hain, kyunki ye 5Y se barabar ka comparison nahi hai. Ek accha saal hone se naya fund upar aa sakta hai, isliye orange wale dhyan se dekho.
  • 1 saal se naye funds rank nahi hote; ye neeche "Rank nahi hue" table mein reason ke saath dikhte hain.
  • Jis fund ka expense ratio ya exit load nahi mila, vo bhi rank nahi hota.
  • Index funds (jaise Nifty Smallcap 250 Index Fund) is list mein nahi aate; AMFI unhe alag category mein rakhta hai.
  • Band ya merge ho chuke funds (jinka NAV 30 din se update nahi hua) aur IDCW/dividend plans hata diye jaate hain.
