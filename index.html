Best SIP Finder

Direct Growth funds ko CAGR (5Y, naye funds ka 3Y/1Y), Exit Load aur Expense Ratio ke rank se sort karta hai.

Upar se category choose karo.
Ranking rules
  • Sirf Direct Plan + Growth funds (AMFI official category list). Index funds is category mein nahi aate.
  • CAGR = NAV aaj vs 5 saal pehle (mfapi.in). Zyada = acha. Fund 5 saal purana nahi to 3Y, woh bhi nahi to 1Y CAGR liya jata hai (orange mein). Ye 5Y se barabar comparison nahi hai. 1 saal se naye fund rank nahi hote.
  • Exit Load: pehle % kam, phir period chhota, phir free units zyada = acha. Nil sabse acha.
  • Expense Ratio: kam = acha.
  • Tie ho to same rank (1, 1, 3). Total = teeno ranks ka sum, sabse kam total = best. Total tie ho to zyada CAGR wala upar.
  • Online version ka data roz raat ~9:30 baje (IST) apne aap update hota hai.
  • Min SIP sirf info ke liye hai, ranking mein nahi gina jata.
  • Exit load / expense ratio / min SIP Groww ke public data se aate hain; invest karne se pehle fund ki site par confirm kar lena. Ye investment advice nahi hai.
